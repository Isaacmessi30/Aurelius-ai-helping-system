# Common questions — draft support guidance

## How do I change startup commands?
Open your server's Startup tab in the Pterodactyl panel. Only fields made editable for your server can be changed. For unavailable options, contact staff.

## Can you restart my server?
This AI bot cannot restart or otherwise modify servers. Customers may use Pterodactyl controls themselves, or ask staff for assistance.

## How do I troubleshoot Discord bots?
Share a redacted console error and mention the programming language, runtime version, and what changed recently. Do not paste Discord tokens or .env values.

## What are the prices or refund rules?
The AI must not invent prices, refunds, or guarantees. Staff can confirm current published plans and applicable policy.

## Is it safe to send my token in a ticket?
No. Never share bot tokens, API keys, SSH private keys or account passwords. Rotate any secrets that were exposed.
